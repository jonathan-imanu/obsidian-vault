# Token

A token represents a transferable unit of value.
- It is a digital asset implemented as a smart contract on the Ethereum blockchain

We have a standard interface for tokens to create predictable APIs for wallets, dApps, and protocols.

## ERC-20 Fungible Tokens

Tokens are fungible meaning that they are interchangable.
- They represent the ownership of a quantity relative to a total supply

**Features:**
1. Allow token holder owner to transfer to a address
2. Or allow the holder to delegate spending to another address via allowances

These are used in Stablecoins.
## ERC-721 Non-Fungible Token (NFT)

Tokens are non-fungible meaning they are all unique and identified by an id.
- This represents the ownership of a unique asset with a collection

**Features**:
- Allow a token holder to transfer ownership of the asset
- Or allow the owner to delegate transfer to another address with an approval
