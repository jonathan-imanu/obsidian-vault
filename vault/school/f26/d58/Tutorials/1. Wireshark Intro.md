Wireshark is an example of a **packet sniffer**.

Packet sniffers have two components:

1. "Sniffing": Using a *packet capture library* to receive a copy of every link-layer frame that is sent by a device over a given interface.
	1. By capturing all link-layer frames, we get all messages sent / received over the monitored link from **all** protocols and applications executing in our computer.
2. "Analyzing": A *packet analyzer* displays the contents of all fields within a protocol message. It must understand the structure of all messages exchanged by the protocols to do this.
